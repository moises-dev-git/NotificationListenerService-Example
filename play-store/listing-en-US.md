# Title
Notilog: Notification History

# Short description
Keep a history of every notification, even messages deleted by the sender.

# Full description
Missed a notification? Someone deleted a message before you could read it? Notilog keeps a history of every notification you receive, so you can read it later.

★ HISTORY
Every notification is saved with the app, title, text and time. Scroll back through days or months of notifications, even after you dismiss them.

★ SEARCH AND FILTER
Find any notification by app, date or text. Delete the results you don't need in one tap.

★ VOICE NOTES THAT DON'T DISAPPEAR
Optionally, Notilog copies each WhatsApp voice note as soon as it arrives, so you can still listen to it if the sender deletes it.

★ YOU DECIDE WHAT IS SAVED
Skip apps, words or phrases you don't want in the history, and automatically clear unwanted notifications from the status bar.

★ EXPORT
Save or share the whole history as a JSON file.

★ PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Everything stays on your device.
• No account, no ads, no analytics.
• No internet permission: your data cannot leave the phone.

Available in English and Portuguese.

HOW IT WORKS
Notilog uses Android's notification access, which you grant once in the system settings. You can revoke it at any time.

Notilog is an independent app and is not affiliated with WhatsApp or Meta.
