# Notilog – Privacy Policy

**English** · [Português](PRIVACY.pt-BR.md)

Effective date: October 27, 2026

Notilog keeps a history of the notifications you receive on your Android device. This policy explains what the app accesses and what it does with it.

## Summary

**All data stays on your device.** Notilog has no servers, no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it does not request internet access. The developer never receives any of your data.

## What the app accesses

- **Notifications** – after you grant *notification access*, Notilog reads each notification posted on your device: the app that sent it, the title, the text and the time. It saves them in a database inside the app's private storage so you can read them later.
- **WhatsApp voice notes (optional)** – only if you turn on *Save incoming voice notes* and select the WhatsApp voice notes folder. Notilog then copies new voice note files from that folder into the app's private storage. It only reads the folder you choose.
- **Your settings** – filters, language and other preferences, stored in the app.

## What the app does not do

- It does not send any data over the internet or to the developer.
- It does not share data with third parties.
- It does not collect location, contacts, identifiers or usage statistics.

## Exporting

When you tap *Save JSON* or *Share* in Settings, Notilog creates a file with your notification history. *Save JSON* writes it to your device's Downloads folder; *Share* hands it to the app you choose. This only happens when you ask for it, and what happens to the file afterwards is up to you.

## Backups

If Android backup is turned on in your device settings, the system may include Notilog's data in your device backup (for example, to your Google account). This is controlled by you in Android's settings, not by Notilog.

## Deleting your data

- Delete notifications in the *Filter* screen and voice notes in the *Voice notes* screen.
- Uninstalling Notilog permanently deletes all of its data from the device.
- You can revoke notification access at any time in Android's settings.

## Children

Notilog is not directed to children under 13.
